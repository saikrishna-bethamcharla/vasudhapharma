What We Do

Products

From complex intermediates to finished APIs, we deliver quality products across therapeutic areas with world-class manufacturing capabilities.

APIs

Total: 55

APIs, or Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients, are the components in a pharmaceutical drug that produce the intended therapeutic effect. Our portfolio spans 40+ APIs manufactured under stringent quality systems for global pharmaceutical companies.

View APIs →

Intermediates

Total: 98

Pharmaceutical intermediates serve as building blocks used during API production. They are used solely in the synthesis of APIs and are not directly administered to patients.

View Intermediates →

Pellets & MUPS

Total: 56

Pellets are small, free-flowing, spherical particles made by combining fine powders or granules of drugs and excipients — value-added, semi-finished formulations for novel drug delivery systems.

View Pellets →

Piperidone & Piperidine Derivatives

Total: 31

Piperidines are among the most important synthetic fragments for designing drugs and play a significant role in the pharmaceutical industry. Cost-effective processes for these derivatives are our core expertise.

View Derivatives →

Under Development APIs

Total: 25

Under-development APIs are driven by pharmaceutical innovation, enabling us to meet dynamic healthcare demands and emerging treatment challenges.
